DOV Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

DOV Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Demek O Yüzden" channel to upload that channel's own videos. This page explains what the tool accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the channel owner's YouTube channel and to set their title, description, category, visibility, publish time and thumbnail.
  • The access and refresh tokens Google issues when the channel owner grants that permission.

The tool does not read viewers' data, comments, subscriptions, watch history or any other account's information.

What is stored

  • The authorization tokens, kept privately by the channel owner so that scheduled uploads can run.
  • A log of the tool's own uploads (video title, date and the resulting video ID).

No personal data about other people is collected, stored or processed.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. The video files and their metadata are sent only to YouTube, which is the purpose of the tool.

Cookies and tracking

The tool has no user interface for the public and places no cookies. This website uses no analytics or tracking.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Once access is revoked the stored tokens stop working, and they are deleted by the channel owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project repository.
